Preferred fiat currency

1. Settings matches the rest of the app
   a. On the main screen, pick a new currency in the LTC price bento (e.g. EUR).
   b. Open Settings. Fiat Currency should show the same currency, and its picker should be on it.
   c. Close and relaunch the app. Settings should still show your currency, not USD.

2. Changing it in Settings
   a. In Settings, expand Fiat Currency and pick another currency (e.g. GBP).
   b. Go back to the main screen. The balance and the LTC price bento should both use GBP.

3. Price pulse while the new currency loads
   a. Pick a new currency in the LTC price bento.
   b. The price should fade in and out quickly until the price in the new currency appears (up to about 20 seconds), then stop and stay fully visible.
   c. Changing the currency in Settings should also make the bento price pulse until the new price shows.
